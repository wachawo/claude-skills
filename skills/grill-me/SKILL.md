---
name: grill-me
description: Mercilessly interrogate the user about their plan or design until shared understanding is reached and every branch of the decision tree is resolved. Use when the user wants to stress-test a plan, be "grilled" on a design, or mentions "grill me".
---

Interrogate me without mercy on every aspect of this plan until we reach shared understanding. Walk through every branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one at a time. For each question, propose your recommended answer.

Ask questions one at a time.

If a question can be answered by examining the codebase, examine the codebase instead of asking.
